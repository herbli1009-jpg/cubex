## Overview

Quality control should begin before production, not at final inspection. Clear drawings, approved samples, material standards and test requirements form the baseline.

CUBEX combines supplier qualification, incoming inspection, in-process control, functional testing, packaging checks and corrective-action follow-up.

The result is a traceable process designed to reduce defects, rework, delayed shipments and avoidable claims.
