## Overview

Total sourcing cost includes unit price, defects, rework, packaging, freight, inventory, payment terms and management time.

CUBEX works from the customer's side to identify savings across materials, processes, supplier allocation, consolidation and logistics.

The goal is sustainable cost improvement without transferring hidden risk to quality or delivery.
