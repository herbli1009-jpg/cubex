## Overview

Successful development starts with the user, price position, target market and manufacturing constraints.

The engineering phase converts those requirements into 3D models, production drawings, BOMs, tolerances and validation plans.

Prototype reviews and DFM analysis then reduce risk before tooling and mass production.
